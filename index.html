Newest upload —
The sender is a small Google Apps Script that runs from your Gmail account. One-time setup:
  1. Open script.google.com → New project. Paste the contents of Code.gs from the apps-script folder, replacing the default code.
  2. Project Settings → tick "Show appsscript.json", then paste the provided appsscript.json over it (sets the Indian time zone and permissions).
  3. Pick the function setup in the toolbar and press Run. Approve the Google permission prompts (Gmail, Sheets, Drive, external requests).
After that it checks every 5 minutes for due schedules and shows up here as "Running".
